    How to play
    
Move to aim and tap or click to drop. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys and space.

    
Two matching toppings merge into the next one in the row above. The signature chili is the hard one to make. Two whole pizzas clear for a big bonus.

    
If anything sits above the dashed line for two seconds, the game ends.
