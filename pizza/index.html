Pizza Drop

Two of the same topping make the next one up. Work your way to a whole pizza before the box overflows.

How to play

On a computer, move the mouse to aim and click to drop (arrow keys and space work too). On a phone, drag to aim and let go to drop.

Two olives make a basil, two basils make a garlic, and so on up the list under the box. You only get the five smallest toppings. Everything bigger, you have to build. The chili is the tough one.

If anything stays above the dashed line for three seconds, you're done.

Sign in with Discord to get on the weekly high scores. They reset Wednesdays at 9am Eastern.
