Squee's House of Pizza

Pizza Drop

Drop toppings. Two of the same merge into the next one up. Build the whole pie before the box overflows.

How to play

Move to aim and tap or click to drop. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys and space.

Two matching toppings merge into the next one up. The merge order is under the box. The signature chili is the hard one to make. Two whole pizzas clear for a big bonus.

If anything sits above the dashed line for two seconds, the game ends.

Sign in with Discord to put your best score on this week's board. It resets every Wednesday at 9am Eastern.
