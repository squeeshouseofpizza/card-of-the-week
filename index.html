    How to play

    Guess any Premodern-legal card. Each guess is compared with this week's card on five things.

    Exact match (marked ✓). Close (marked ≈): same block for sets, one away for mana value, a shared color or card type. Arrows point toward the answer.

    Set means the card's first printing between Fourth Edition and Scourge, so Swords to Plowshares counts as Fourth Edition. The artist unlocks after three misses and the card's art after four.

    A new card comes out every Wednesday at 9am Eastern. You can play the last four weeks for practice; only the current week counts toward your streak and the leaderboard, and your streak survives one skipped week.

    The leaderboard ranks this week's solvers who are signed in with Discord: fewest guesses first, then fastest time from first guess to last. Signing in shows your Discord display name and picture on the board. You can play without signing in.
