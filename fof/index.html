How to play
  1. Fact means it happened on the show. Fiction means it didn’t. Drag the card left for Fact or right for Fiction, or tap a button.
  2. Then pick how sure you are. “Lock it in”: +1 if right, 0 if wrong. “I’m sure”: +2 if right, −2 if wrong. Only worth it if you’d be right 2 times in 3.
  3. Each card flips to show what was really said, with a link to that moment in the episode.
  4. Sign in with Discord after you play to put your score on the board.
